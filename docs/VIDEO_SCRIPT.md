# MeetMind Demo Video Script (90 Seconds)

**Video Title:** MeetMind: Your Meetings Are Remembered | Hindsight AI Agent  
**Video Description:** MeetMind is an AI meeting memory agent powered by Hindsight Cloud and Google Gemini. Watch how conversation notes are transformed into persistent intelligence that prepares you for every upcoming client meeting.  
**Thumbnail Text:** MeetMind · "Your meetings are remembered." (Badge: Powered by Hindsight)  

---

### Screen Recording Checklist:
- [ ] Browser window sized to clean 1080p (1920x1080)
- [ ] Dark mode/light mode high contrast
- [ ] Open on Dashboard (`Good morning, Alex 👋`)
- [ ] Clear mouse movements, no frantic clicking
- [ ] Have sample meeting notes ready for Sarah Mitchell or use the 1-click pre-fill button

---

### Timing & Narration Breakdown:

#### 0:00 – 0:10 | The Problem
- **Visual:** Split screen or quick animation showing scattered notes in Slack, Notion, and email threads, followed by an awkward meeting screen asking: *"Wait, what was your budget again?"*
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"We’ve all had that sinking feeling before a meeting. What did the client say their budget was? What technology did they insist on? What was their biggest deadline fear? Ordinary AI tools forget everything the moment the meeting ends."*

#### 0:10 – 0:20 | Introducing MeetMind
- **Visual:** Zoom into MeetMind clean dashboard. Brand logo with tagline: *"Your meetings are remembered."*
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"Meet MeetMind — an AI meeting intelligence agent powered by Hindsight persistent memory. MeetMind turns every conversation into context for the next one."*

#### 0:20 – 0:35 | Dashboard & Client Context
- **Visual:** Navigate to Dashboard. Point out the KPI stats: Total Clients (12), Meetings (36), Hindsight Memories (128). Highlight the upcoming meeting card: *Sarah Mitchell, Product Director at NovaTech Solutions, Today at 3:00 PM*.
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"Here on the dashboard, we see our upcoming meeting with Sarah Mitchell today at 3:00 PM. Notice the memory counter: MeetMind has already retained 8 crucial facts across our previous discussions."*

#### 0:35 – 0:50 | Adding Notes & AI Analysis
- **Visual:** Click "Add Meeting". Select Sarah Mitchell. Click the 1-click script button "Load Sarah Mitchell Scope Demo". Click "Save Meeting & Learn".
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"When you finish a meeting, just paste your raw conversation notes. Watch what happens when we click 'Save Meeting & Learn'. Google Gemini analyzes the notes, while Hindsight extracts and retains structured memory units."*

#### 0:50 – 1:00 | Hindsight Memory Learned Celebration
- **Visual:** The screen smoothly updates to the celebratory card:  
  *🧠 MeetMind learned 5 new memories.*  
  Show the checkmarks:  
  ✓ Client prefers React  
  ✓ Budget is ₹15 lakh ceiling  
  ✓ Target launch is December  
  ✓ Delivery timeline is primary concern  
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"Instantly, MeetMind confirms: 5 new memories retained into our isolated Hindsight bank: preferences, budget caps, and critical risk factors."*

#### 1:00 – 1:15 | The Flagship "Prepare Me"
- **Visual:** Click the flagship button: **"Prepare Me"**. Show Hindsight multi-strategy recall executing.
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"Now, imagine it’s 2:55 PM. You click 'Prepare Me'. Hindsight instantly recalls all historical facts across every previous meeting to assemble an executive briefing."*

#### 1:15 – 1:25 | Personalized Output & Follow-up
- **Visual:** Scroll down the personalized Meeting Brief. Show *Strategic Objective*, *What You Should Remember*, *Important Concerns*, *Suggested Questions*, and click *Draft Follow-Up Email*.
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"No generic advice here. MeetMind explicitly reminds us: Sarah prefers React, has a ₹15 lakh ceiling, and is worried about the December freeze. It even drafts our personalized follow-up email in seconds."*

#### 1:25 – 1:30 | Closing & Value Proposition
- **Visual:** Return to hero screen with brand badge.
- **Voiceover (Narration):**  
  *"MeetMind doesn't just answer you — it remembers your conversations and gets better prepared for the next one."*
